🌱 Welcome to Brave Steps

  • Log an outing in a few taps. Rate anxiety from 0 (calm) to 10 (most scared) at each step: before leaving, in the car, during, after.
  • See progress over time. Charts show whether peaks are coming down.
  • Install it: iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu → Add to Home screen.

This is a beta tracking tool, not medical advice or treatment. Use it alongside the plan from your child's or your own therapist.
